## Architecture and implementation limits

- Compiler: Kotlin with ANTLR4. VM and Runtime: C++.
  Other components: Go or Rust. The eventual application: the custom `.tc` language.
- The Broker routes messages but does not aggregate targets.
- The DBMS parses and executes SQL but does not know the Target domain.
- The detector exposes marker IDs, angles and distances; Analyzer computes Observation.
- Public interface changes require an approved contract in `spec/` and reviews
  from the affected owners.
- Unresolved decisions are tracked in `docs/ai-context/02-open-questions.md`.
